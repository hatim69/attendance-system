Privacy policy

Attendance app of {{COMPANY}} · last updated October 2026

This app is used by employees of {{COMPANY}} ("the company") to record attendance, apply for leave and work from home, and download their payslips. It is not a public app: accounts are created by the company's HR team, and only employees can sign in. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

What the app collects

The app does not track your location in the background, does not read your contacts, files, messages or other apps, and does not use advertising or analytics services. The camera and location are used only while the app is open and you tap the check-in button, and only after you allow them.

Who can see it

Your data is stored on the company's own server and is visible only to authorised people at the company: HR, management, administrators and your team lead (team leads see only their own team). The company does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone outside the company, except where required by law.

How long it is kept

Attendance, leave and payroll records are kept for as long as you work at the company and afterwards for as long as needed for payroll, accounting and legal record-keeping. Selfie photos are kept as proof of attendance and may be deleted by the company once they are no longer needed.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). PINs and passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Every change made by HR or management is recorded in an audit log.

Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse camera or location permission; you will then not be able to check in from the app (the fingerprint machine still works).
  • You can ask HR to see, correct or delete your data, subject to the company's legal record-keeping duties.
  • Your app account is removed when you leave the company.
